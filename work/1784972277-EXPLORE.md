## Part 2 — NEW node `[SHIFT-DIM1]` (the valuable consequence)

**Statement.** The twin prime problem admits a **sieve-dimension-1 formulation**. Taking
`a_n = Λ(n−2)`, Bombieri's asymptotic sieve applies **verbatim** under EH: its hypothesis (Type I at
every level `X^{1−ε}`) *is* Elliott–Halberstam (`L6`), its dimension requirement is met (`L5`), and its
constant is `2Π₂` (`L7`). Hence, **conditionally on EH**,

    Σ_{n ≤ x} Λ(n−2) Λ_k(n)  ~  k · 2Π₂ · x (log x)^{k−1}        for every k ≥ 2,

with the case `k = 1` — which is TPC — failing for exactly one reason: parity.

**Why this matters to the project.** The project's central claim has always been that the difficulty
*concentrates* on parity, and the paper is careful to call that reduction **heuristic at the relevant
sieve dimension**. `[SHIFT-DIM1]` gives a formulation in which "everything except parity is available"
is a **theorem modulo EH**, not a heuristic — the `k ≥ 2` cases are delivered and `k = 1` is not.

**Audit (numerical consistency, `k = 2`).** Predicted ratio `2H = 2.6406`.

```
   x            S/(x log x)     twin part     almost-prime part
     100000       2.3172          1.2015           1.1157
     500000       2.3511          1.2089           1.1422
    1000000       2.3619          1.2177           1.1441
    3000000       2.3923          1.2380           1.1543
    5999999       2.4059          1.2411           1.1648
```

At `x = 6·10⁶`, `log x ≈ 15.6`, and the shortfall (2.406 vs 2.641, −8.9%) is of size `1/log x` — the
expected secondary term. The two parts split roughly evenly and both climb toward `H = 1.3203`, which is
the parity phenomenon itself (primes and 2-almost-primes carry equal weight in `Λ₂`).
**This is consistency, not verification** — the statement is asymptotic and conditional on EH.

**NOVELTY NOT CLAIMED.** A conditional Bombieri corollary of this shape is plausibly known. It must be
checked against the literature before any claim is made. *(Recorded per the A22/A23 lessons.)*
